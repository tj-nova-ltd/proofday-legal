Terms of use

The deal between you and Proofday, in plain words.

Last updated 1 November 2026

  1. Your plans, your call

    You choose every plan, its criteria and its timing. Once saved, the time and criteria are locked so the plan means something; you can turn reminders off or exit a plan at any time, and the exit is recorded honestly.

  2. What a review can and cannot say

    A photo can show a visible result. It cannot prove who did the work, and automated review can be wrong. If the result is unclear, Proofday asks for another photo instead of guessing.

  3. Fair use

    Only submit photos you have the right to take and share. Do not submit photos of other people without their permission or anything unlawful.

  4. Availability

    Notifications depend on iOS limits, permissions and Focus settings. Network or service problems may delay a review; your plan stays pending until it can be reviewed.

    Proofday is free to download and use. There are no in-app purchases in this version.
