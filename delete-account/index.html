Delete your account

How to remove your Proofday account and every photo you sent.

Last updated 1 November 2026

  1. In the app (fastest)

    Open Proofday → Me → Privacy & data → Delete account → Delete. This permanently deletes your account, all plans, submitted photos and sessions on every device. If you are not signed in, the same place reads “Delete all data on this iPhone”.

  2. Without the app

    Email support@tjnovaltd.com from the address you sign in with, subject “Proofday account deletion”. We complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm by email.

  3. What is kept

    Nothing tied to your account is kept after deletion, except records we are legally required to retain.
