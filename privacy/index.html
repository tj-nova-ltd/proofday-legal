Privacy

What Proofday keeps on your iPhone, what leaves it, and how to erase it.

Last updated 1 November 2026

  1. On your iPhone

    Plans you create (title, start and due time, the visible criteria you set, quiet hours, time zone and status), your reminder setting and your photo-sending choice are stored on this iPhone. You can use plans and reminders without an account.

  2. Photos you choose to send

    Proofday only opens the camera when you tap to capture inside a plan, and never reads your photo library. A photo leaves the device only if you have switched on “Send my photos for review” and then submit that photo. It goes to the Proofday review service, which checks the visible result against the criteria you wrote and returns a pass or a request for another photo.

    Image review is performed by a third-party vision AI service that the Proofday review server calls through an OpenAI-compatible API. No vision service is enabled at present: until one is, no photo is sent to any AI service and plans stay awaiting review. We will name the service on this page before enabling it. Photos submitted to the review server are kept for 7 days and then deleted automatically.

  3. Signing in

    Signing in is optional and uses your email address and a one-time code. We keep your email address, an account identifier, your device sessions and the plans you sync. We do not ask for a password.

  4. Reminders

    Reminders are local iPhone notifications scheduled on the device. No advertising or tracking SDKs are included, and we do not sell or share personal information.

  5. Deleting everything

    Signed in: Me → Privacy & data → Delete account permanently removes your Proofday account, all plans, submitted photos and sessions on every device. Not signed in: Me → Privacy & data → Delete all data on this iPhone removes everything stored locally. You can also email us (see Support).
